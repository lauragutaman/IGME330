About this app

  • The pulldown menu above allows the user to change the divergence angle
  • The "Restart App" button will restore the app to n=1 and to the inital petal size and padding values. The app will use the currently selected divergence angle
  • Changes made from starter code:
    • Pulldown menu changes the angle of the floret, change happens while the floret is drawing
    • Clicking the canvas starts a new in the spot the user clicks
    • Raised the frame rate to 60 FPS and replaced magic numbers (fps, dot radius, padding, color, speed, and growth rate)
    • Once the flower has completely off the canvas, the screen will automatically restart itself
